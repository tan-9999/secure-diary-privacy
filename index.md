SECURE DIARY: PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 21/11/2026

Secure Diary is a private diary app for Android, made by Tanmay Shimpi.
Contact: tanmays9999@gmail.com

THE SHORT VERSION
Your diary stays on your phone, encrypted. The app has no account system, no ads,
no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any server that receives your data.

WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR PHONE
- Your entries, titles, tags, moods and dates
- Photos you attach, if any
- A location (coordinates) on an entry, only if you tap "Add current location"
- Your settings, and a salted hash of your PIN or pattern (never the PIN itself)
All of this is encrypted on your phone. The encryption key is held in the
Android Keystore. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by Android; the app
never sees your biometric data.

OPTIONAL: GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP
If you turn on backup, the app connects to your Google account using Google
Sign-In, and uploads a backup file to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
- The file is encrypted on your phone, with a passphrase only you know, before it
  leaves your phone.
- The app can only see its own hidden folder. It cannot see your other Drive files.
- We cannot read your backups, and we cannot recover your passphrase. If you forget
  it, your backups cannot be opened.
- The app stores your Google account email on your phone, to show which account is
  connected. It does not store Google access tokens.
- Google's own privacy policy applies to your use of Google Drive and Google Sign-In.
You can delete backups inside the app, or in your Google account's Drive settings,
and you can disconnect at any time.

OPTIONAL: LOCATION, CAMERA AND PHOTOS
The app asks for access only when you use the related feature. Location is read
once, when you tap "Add current location", rounded to about 11 metres, and saved with
that entry on your phone. It is not looked up on a map service.

EXPORT
You can export your diary as a ZIP of Markdown files, photos and JSON. The exported file
is NOT encrypted. You choose where it is saved or sent. Keep it safe.

SHARING
We do not sell, share or send your diary data to anyone. The app contains no
advertising or analytics code. Google Play may show us anonymous crash and performance
reports from users who have chosen to share them with Google.

DELETING YOUR DATA
In Settings, "Delete all data" removes everything on your phone and, if you choose, your
backups in Google Drive. Uninstalling the app also removes the data on your phone.

SECURITY AND LIMITS
We use encryption and the phone's secure key storage, but no system is perfectly secure.
A forgotten PIN, pattern or backup passphrase cannot be recovered. Keep your phone's own
lock screen on, and keep your backup passphrase somewhere safe.

CHILDREN
Secure Diary is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country).

CHANGES
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the
app's release notes.
